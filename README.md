# Gachibowli & Kokapet property map

A single-page map of property listings in **Gachibowli** and **Kokapet** (Hyderabad), within 9 km of Infosys, Gachibowli.

Open the site: `index.html` (published with GitHub Pages).

## What the distances are

| Area | Source |
|---|---|
| Gachibowli | Google Maps best-route distances collected per property: Infosys, metro, large company, ORR, 15 hospitals, 26 schools, 15 parks |
| Kokapet | ORR connectivity, airport distance and nearby places collected from Google Maps per project; Infosys, metro and school/hospital distances from Mapbox driving routes |

Places of interest and the straight-line "nearby places" lists come from OpenStreetMap.
Listing details come from public property portals and may contain errors (some sizes and prices look wrong).

The page is generated by the build scripts in the `infosys-area-clusters` project (`build/inject.py`); do not edit `index.html` by hand.
